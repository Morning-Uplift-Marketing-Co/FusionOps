📈 RISK SCORING SYSTEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Components (each 0-100 where 100 = safest):
  • proxy_risk         (from ARGUS)
  • isolation_score    (from NEXUS)
  • traffic_quality    (from IRIS)
  • timeline_risk      (from CHRONO)

Final Score: Weighted average → verdict_score (0-100)
             Higher = More risk (inverted scale)

Status Mapping:
  0-30   → 🟢 HEALTHY   (Safe)
  31-60  → 🟡 WATCH     (Monitor closely)
  61-80  → 🔴 RISK      (Action required)
  81-100 → 🔴 CRITICAL  (Pause account)

Auto-Pause Rules:
  ✓ critical_risk_pause (score ≥80)  → PAUSE
  ✓ high_risk_reduce    (score ≥60)  → Reduce budget 50%
  ✓ overspend_alert     (spend≥110%) → Alert
  ✓ conv_drop_alert     (drop≥30%)   → Alert
  ✓ creative_fatigue    (score≥70)   → Alert
